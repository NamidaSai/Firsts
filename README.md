# Firsts

**What does it feel like, that first time?**  
Use your mouse to interact.

A short poetic game by **Geoffrey Mugford**  
Made in 24 hours in July–August 2025  
Official Selection – *Game Poems Magazine #1*

---

## About the Game

**Firsts** is a small, quiet game about the emotional texture of beginning.  
It tells its story not through dialogue or text, but through **movement, shape, and gesture** — each interaction a small act of discovery.

The game unfolds in three abstract vignettes, each evoking a different kind of "first step":

- **Departure** — leaving what you know behind  
- **Defiance** — resisting what pushes back  
- **Return** — coming home changed

There are no explicit choices, only subtle shifts. You may not even know you’ve decided something, only that something feels different.  
That moment of *"I think I just did something"* is what the game is designed to preserve.

---

## Artist Statement

> *"With no words and limited visual detail, the challenge became: how do you communicate story, identity, and transformation using only shape and motion? The result is something like a playable haiku: short, sparse, and open to interpretation."*

**Firsts** was made as an experiment in poetic form, exploring how minimal input — moving, clicking, holding — can carry emotional weight when placed in the right context.

Every interaction is deliberately underexplained, inviting you to bring your own meaning and project your own story. The game is about moving forward, hesitating, and offering something of yourself when you return — hoping to be recognised, even if you’re no longer quite the same shape.

## Play

You can play **Firsts** for free on [itch.io](https://namidasai.itch.io/firsts).

- **Platform:** WebGL (playable in-browser)  
- **Controls:** Mouse only (move, click, hold)
